# 🎙️ NOX · Vox & Note

Ever left a meeting wondering, “Wait, what did we agree to do by Friday?” Give NOX the recording and ask. You can turn the same recording into a recap, a translation, or a to-do list.

## Try it with a group meeting

1. **Capture the conversation.** Record in your browser or upload an audio file. Preview it, then turn it into text.
2. **Find the details.** Summarize the discussion, clean up the transcript into readable notes, or ask “Who’s sending the slides?”
3. **Follow up.** Pull out action items, give them deadlines, and set a reminder before the next meeting.

## What can NOX do?

| Feature | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| 🎤 **Record or upload** | Use your microphone and watch the live waveform, or upload a recording and preview it. |
| 📝 **Transcribe** | Turn audio into editable text with ElevenLabs. Send the transcript to the summary, translation, to-do, or Q&A tools. |
| ✨ **Summarize and polish** | Get a short recap you can copy, or tidy up the full transcript into readable notes. |
| 💬 **Ask about the transcript** | Ask questions such as “What did we decide?” or “When is the deadline?” NOX uses the transcript to answer. |
| 🌍 **Translate** | Translate with MyMemory into Burmese, English, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Hindi, or Thai. Copy the original or the translation. |
| ✅ **Make a to-do list** | Pull tasks from a transcript or add your own. Check them off, delete them, see your progress, and filter by status. |
| 🔔 **Set reminders** | Give a task a deadline or create a standalone reminder. Use the sidebar bell to edit or delete reminders, and choose whether to be alerted at the deadline or earlier. Browser notifications depend on permission and the notification service. |
| 🔊 **Listen to text** | Choose a language and playback speed, then play, pause, resume, or stop. Replay or download saved speech audio from history. |
| 🗂️ **Revisit your work** | Search or filter your activity history. Open an entry to see the original input and result, copy a result, or delete entries individually or in bulk. |
| 👤 **Manage your session** | Create an account, sign in, open your profile, and sign out. |
| 🇲🇲 **Switch interface languages** | Choose English or Burmese (မြန်မာ) from your profile. |
